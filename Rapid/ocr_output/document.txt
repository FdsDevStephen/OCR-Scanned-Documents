The petitioners most respectfully submits as hereunder:
1. It is respectfully submitted that the Petitioners belong to an agrarian family, whose sole source of livelihood is agricultural cultivation. The Petitioners and their predecessors-in-title have been in continuous, uninterrupted, and peaceful possession and cultivation of the land bearing Survey No. 157, measuring an extent of 2 Acres, situated at Block No. I & II, Melekalahalli Village, Kadaba Hobli, Gubbi Taluk, Tumakuru District, for the past five decades. The Petitioners' late father, Sri Kalaiah S/o Beluraiah, was in actual and exclusive possession and personal cultivation of the said land for several decades. Recognizing his longstanding possession, bona fide cultivation, and genuine entitlement, the Bagur Hukum Saguvali Committee, acting under the socio-economic land grant scheme framed by the Government of Karnataka for the benefit of depressed and backward classes, considered his application.
2. It is submitted that, Pursuant to a detailed enquiry, spot inspection, and based on favourable reports submitted by the Village Accountant

—6- and the jurisdictional Tahsildar, the Committee was pleased to recommend the grant of 2 Acres of land in favour of Sri Kalaiah, under the Depressed Class category. Accordingly, the competent authority — the Tahsildar, Gubbi Taluk — issued an Official Memorandum dated 22.11.2017, bearing reference LND. RUC.(KD)CR.135-1991-92, confirming the grant of the said land. The said Official Memorandum explicitly mentions the name of Sri Kalaiah, his caste classification, and the precise extent and location of land granted, i. e., 2 Acres in Block Nos. I & II, situated in the aforeméntioned Survey Number and village. A true copy of the said Official Memorandum dated 22.11.2017 is produced herewith and marked as ANNEXURE-A.
3. It is further submitted that, pursuant to the favourable recommendation of the Bagur Hukum Saguvali Committee, a resolution was duly passed by the competent Land Grant Committee, granting an extent of 2 Acres of land in favour of Sri Kalaiah S/o Beluraiah, recognizing his longstanding cultivation and entitlement under the socio-beneficiary scheme for members of the Depressed Class. Consequently, in implementation of the said resolution, the jurisdictional Tahsildar of Gubbi Taluk, having conducted a detailed verification of records and personal enquiry, proceeded to formally issue a Saguvali Chit in the name of Sri Kalaiah, thereby vesting lawful title and possession over the granted land.
The Saguvali Chit, dated 01.01.2018, was issued in conformity with the Official Memorandum and in accordance with the prescribed rules governing land grants to beneficiaries under such schemes. A true and

1一 correct copy of the said Saguvali Chit dated 01.01.2018 is produced herewith and marked as ANNEXURE-B.
4. It is respectfully submitted that, pursuant to the issuance of the Official Memorandum and the subsequent Saguvali Chit in favour of the Petitioners’ father, the Tahsildar, Gubbi Taluk, in coordination with the survey officials, conducted a spot inspection and survey of the granted land to demarcate the boundaries and ascertain the exact physical possession. Accordingly, a detailed land sketch was prepared on 18.09.2024, reflecting the physical location, measurement, and demarcation of the granted land. The said sketch clearly identifies and delineates 1 Acre each in Block-I and Block-II of Survey No.157, situated at Melekalahalli Village, Kadaba Hobli, Gubbi Taluk, as being in the lawful possession and cultivation of the Petitioners. A true and correct copy of the Sketch dated 19.09.2024 is produced herewith and marked as ANNEXURE-C.
5. It is submitted that, following the grant of land and issuance of the Saguvali Chit, the jurisdictional Revenue Authorities, upon conducting due verification and enquiry, were pleased to effect mutation in the revenue records in respect of the subject land.
Accordingly, the name of the Petitioners' father, Sri Kalaiah, was duly incorporated in the Record of Rights, Tenaney and Crops (RTC) pertaining to Survey No.157, measuring 2 Acres situated at Block-I and II, Melekalahalli Village, Kadaba Hobli, Gubbi Taluk, thereby conferring legal recognition of his title and possession over the granted land. True copies of the Mutation Register (M. R.) entry and corresponding RTC extracts evidencing such incorporation are

-8一 produced herewith and marked as ANNEXURES-D and D1, respectively.
6. It is further submitted that the Petitioners have, in continuation of their lawful possession and enjoyment of the said land, regularly remitted land revenue/tax to the concerned revenue authorities. Few of the relevant tax paid receipts evidencing such remittance are produced herewith and marked as ANNEXURES-E and E1.
7. It is respectfully submitted that, in the manner stated above, the Petitioners and their family have been in lawful, continuous, and peaceful possession and personal cultivation of the granted land, raising seasonal agricultural crops such as Ragi, Maize, and other dry- land crops, without interruption or interference from any quarter.
However, to the Petitioners’ shock and dismay, it came to their notice that the Assistant Director of Land Records (ADLR), acting in vested interest and without due authority, unilaterally undertook Phodi and Durasth operations (sub-division and boundary marking) on the subject land, without issuing any prior notice or providing an opportunity of hearing to the Petitioners, as required under law.
Pursuant to such unauthorized action, the ADLR communicated the outcome of the Phodi and Durasth exercise to the jurisdictional Tahsildar, who, in blatant violation of the principles of natural justice, proceeded to reduce an extent of 0.24 guntas from the land granted to the Petitioners and arbitrarily added the same to the landholding of another individual, allegedly favouring such third party without any legal basis.

_a-
8. It is pertinent to note that the very same Tahsildar, who had previously conducted the spot inspection and prepared the valid land sketch dated 19.09.2024, now, acting without jurisdiction and under apparent extrancous influence, prepared a second sketch unilaterally, thereby diminishing the Petitioners’ landholding by 0.24 guntas, without issuing any notice or order under due process of law. Copies of the communication dated 18.02.2025 from the ADLR and the sketch dated 18.05.2025, evidencing the illegal reduction of land and reallocation, are produced herewith and marked as ANNEXURES-F and G, respectively.
9. It is further submitted that, acting solely on the basis of the communication issued by the Assistant Director of Land Records
(ADLR) and without issuing any prior notice or affording an opportunity of hearing to the Petitioners, the Tahsildar of Gubbi Taluk, in a patently arbitrary and illegal manner, proceeded to pass a unilateral order, thereby reducing an extent of 0.24 guntas from the land earlier granted to the Petitioners’ father. It is most pertinent to bring to the attention of this Hon'ble Court that the very same Tahsildar, who had earlier issued the Official Memorandum dated 22.11.2017 and the Saguvali Chit dated 01.01.2018 and Sketch dated 19.09.2024 in favour of the Petitioners’ father after due enquiry and verification, has now, without any statutory authority or jurisdiction, passed an impugned order bearing No. TOGUB- ADMOLNDG/421/2025 dated 15.05.2025, thereby unlawfully curtailing the extent of the granted land.

—10- Such action is wholly without jurisdiction, amounts to reviewing or annulling a concluded grant order without recourse to any lawful procedure, and is in gross violation of the principles of natural justice and established norms under the Karnataka Land Revenue Act and Rules. A true copy of the impugned Order dated 15.05.2025 passed by the Tahsildar is produced herewith and marked as ANNEXURE-H. It is in the backdrop of this unlawful, arbitrary and high-handed action, and the inaction on the part of the Respondent authorities in redressing the Petitioners' grievances, that the present Writ Petition is being filed, seeking the intervention of this Hon'ble Court on the following, among other, grounds.
10. The petitioner has not filed any other petition before this Hon'ble Court, earlier, on which this petition is filed and there are no proceedings pending in any other Court in respect of the subject matter of this petition. And the petitioner have no other alternative remedy, except to approach this Hon’ble Court.
GROUNDS
11. It is respectfully submitted that the Tahsildar, being a quasi- judicial authority under the Karnataka Land Revenue Act, does not possess inherent or statutory power to review, modify, or annul its own order unilaterally. The Tahsildar had earlier issued the Official Memorandum, Saguvali Chit, and Sketch based on the lawful order passed by the competent Land Grant Committee. Unless such order a u upt i it au sity under the provisions of the Karnataka Land Revenue Act, the Tahsildar is without jurisdiction to alter or nullify the same by passing

—11- a fresh order. In the absence of any statutory power of review or revision conferred on the Tahsildar, the impugned order passed by the Tahsildar is ultra vires, void ab initio, and non est in law. The impugned order passed by the Tahsildar reducing 0.24 guntas of land from the Petitioners' granted landholding has been issued without issuing any prior notice or providing an opportunity of hearing, in gross violation of the principles of natural justice. Any order affecting vested rights must be preceded by notice and hearing. Thus calls for interference liable to set-aside at the hands of this Hon’ble Court.
12. It is respectfully submitted that the Assistant Director of Land Records (ADLR), in complete disregard to statutory mandates and without adhering to the principles of natural justice, conducted Phodi and Durasth operations (sub-division and boundary -demarcation) without issuing any notice or affording an opportunity of hearing to the Petitioners. Acting unilaterally, the ADLR prepared a revised sketch, whereby an extent of 0.24 guntas was illegally reduced from the Petitioners’ granted land and added to the holding of an adjacent landowner, ostensibly to favour such third party.
13. It is most pertinent to state that the ADLR has no statutory authority or jurisdiction under the Karnataka Land Revenue Act, 1964 or the relevant rules to alter, amend, or reduce the extent of granted land in possession of a beneficiary, especially when such grant is supported by an Official Memorandum and a valid Saguvali Chit. The unauthorized alteration of land boundaries, based on a one-sided sketch prepared without legal sanction, is void ab initio and contrary to established legal procedures.

—12-
14. The justification assigned in the communication by the ADLR— that the disputed extent overlaps with a “road” is factually incorrect and legally untenable. The original grant and sketch clearly indicate the existence of a road on the eastern boundary of the land, which has been appropriately accounted for at the time of grant. Therefore, any subsequent claim regarding the existence of a road encroaching upon the granted land is clearly an afterthought, concocted to deprive the Petitioners of their legitimate rights. Moreover, the Tahsildar, relying solely on the said arbitrary communication from the ADLR, passed an order on 15.05.2025 reducing the extent of land held by the Petitioners without jurisdiction, and without following due process of law, which renders the impugned action arbitrary, illegal, and unconstitutional, warranting interference by this Hon’ble Court.
15. The Respondents, Assistant Director of Land Records (ADLR) and the Tahsildar, have ignored these statutory safeguards and, without issuing any notice or affording the Petitioners a hearing, have unilaterally passed an order affecting the Petitioners' vested rights.
Such conduct is a blatant violation of the principles of natural justice and the statutory scheme. Hence, the impugned action calls for interference by this Hon'ble Court, and the Petitioners pray that the Court be pleased to quash the said order and pass such other appropriate reliefs in the interest of justice.
16. It is respectfully submitted that the Petitioners and their family members have been in peaceful possession and enjoyment of the agricultural property measuring 2 Acres in Survey No. 157 for over five decades. The Tahsildar himseff, upon spot inspection, prepared a

—13- detailed sketch on 18.09.2024 clearly identifying the Petitioners’ possession and extent of 2 Acres in Blocks I and II. Despite such settled possession and official recognition, the Tahsildar, influenced by vested interests, has unilaterally passed the impugned order reducing the Petitioners' land without due process or notice. Such arbitrary and illegal action seriously affects the Petitioners’ right to livelihood and peaceful enjoyment of property, and therefore, calls for immediate interference by this Hon’ble Court by way of appropriate writ, order, or direction to protect the Petitioners’ rights and interests.

In the event of not granting interim prayer, more hardship and inconvenience would be caused to the petitioner. Per contra no hardship would be caused to the Respondents, to meet the ends of'justice.
