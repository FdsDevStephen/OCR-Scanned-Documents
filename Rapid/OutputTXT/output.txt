MEMORANDUM OF WRIT PETITION UNDER ARTICLE 226
& 227 OF THE CONSTITUTION OF INDIA.
The petitioners most respectfully submits as hereunder:
1. It is respectfully submitted that the Petitioners belong to an agrarian
family, whose sole source of livelihood is agricultural cultivation. The
Petitioners and their predecessors-in-title have been in continuous,
uninterrupted, and peaceful possession and cultivation of the land
bearing Survey No. 157, measuring an extent of 2 Acres, situated at
Block No. I & II, Melekalahalli Village, Kadaba Hobli, Gubbi Taluk,
Tumakuru District, for the past five decades. The Petitioners' late
father, Sri Kalaiah S/o Beluraiah, was in actual and exclusive
possession and personal cultivation of the said land for several
decades. Recognizing his longstanding possession, bona fide
cultivation, and genuine entitlement, the Bagur Hukum Saguvali
Committee, acting under the socio-economic land grant scheme
framed by the Government of Karnataka for the benefit of depressed
and backward classes, considered his application.
2. It is submitted that, Pursuant to a detailed enquiry, spot inspection,
and based on favourable reports submitted by the Village Accountant

—6-
and the jurisdictional Tahsildar, the Committee was pleased to
recommend the grant of 2 Acres of land in favour of Sri Kalaiah,
under the Depressed Class category. Accordingly, the competent
authority — the Tahsildar, Gubbi Taluk — issued an Official
Memorandum
dated
22.11.2017,
bearing
reference
LND.RUC.(KD)CR.135-1991-92, confirming the grant of the said
land. The said Official Memorandum explicitly mentions the name of
Sri Kalaiah, his caste classification, and the precise extent and location
of land granted, i.e., 2 Acres in Block Nos. I & II, situated in the
aforementioned Survey Number and village. A true copy of the said
Official Memorandum dated 22.11.2017 is produced herewith and
marked as ANNEXURE-A.
3. It is further submitted that, pursuant to the favourable
recommendation of the Bagur Hukum Saguvali Committee, a
resolution was duly passed by the competent Land Grant Committee,
granting an extent of 2 Acres of land in favour of Sri Kalaiah S/o
Beluraiah, recognizing his longstanding cultivation and entitlement
under the socio-beneficiary scheme for members of the Depressed
Class. Consequently, in implementation of the said resolution, the
jurisdictional Tahsildar of Gubbi Taluk, having conducted a detailed
verification of records and personal enquiry, proceeded to formally
issue a Saguvali Chit in the name of Sri Kalaiah, thereby vesting
lawful title and possession over the granted land.
The Saguvali Chit, dated 01.01.2018, was issued in conformity with
the Official Memorandum and in accordance with the prescribed rules
governing land grants to beneficiaries under such schemes. A true and

7一
correct copy of the said Saguvali Chit dated 01.01.2018 is produced
herewith and marked as ANNEXURE-B.
4. It is respectfully submitted that, pursuant to the issuance of the
Official Memorandum and the subsequent Saguvali Chit in favour of
the Petitioners’ father, the Tahsildar, Gubbi Taluk, in coordination
with the survey officials, conducted a spot inspection and survey of
the granted land to demarcate the boundaries and ascertain the exact
physical possession. Accordingly, a detailed land sketch was prepared
on 18.09.2024, reflecting the physical location, measurement, and
demarcation of the granted land. The said sketch clearly identifies and
delineates 1 Acre each in Block-I and Block-II of Survey No.157,
situated at Melekalahalli Village, Kadaba Hobli, Gubbi Taluk, as
being in the lawful possession and cultivation of the Petitioners. A
true and correct copy of the Sketch dated 19.09.2024 is produced
herewith and marked as ANNEXURE-C.
5. It is submitted that, following the grant of land and issuance of the
Saguvali Chit, the jurisdictional Revenue Authorities, upon
conducting due verification and enquiry, were pleased to effect
mutation in the revenue records in respect of the subject land.
Accordingly, the name of the Petitioners' father, Sri Kalaiah, was duly
incorporated in the Record of Rights, Tenancy and Crops (RTC)
pertaining to Survey No.157, measuring 2 Acres situated at Block-I
and II, Melekalahalli Village, Kadaba Hobli, Gubbi Taluk, thereby
conferring legal recognition of his title and possession over the
granted land. True copies of the Mutation Register (M.R.) entry and
corresponding RTC extracts evidencing such incorporation are

-8一
produced herewith and marked as ANNEXURES-D and D1,
respectively.
6. It is further submitted that the Petitioners have, in continuation of
their lawful possession and enjoyment of the said land, regularly
remitted land revenue/tax to the concerned revenue authorities. Few of
the relevant tax paid receipts evidencing such remittance are produced
herewith and marked as ANNEXURES-E and E1.
7. It is respectfully submitted that, in the manner stated above, the
Petitioners and their family have been in lawful, continuous, and
peaceful possession and personal cultivation of the granted land,
raising seasonal agricultural crops such as Ragi, Maize, and other dry-
land erops, without interruption or interference from any quarter.
However, to the Petitioners’ shock and dismay, it came to their notice
that the Assistant Director of Land Records (ADLR), acting in vested
interest and without due authority, unilaterally undertook Phodi and
Durasth operations (sub-division and boundary marking) on the
subject land, without issuing any prior notice or providing an
opportunity of hearing to the Petitioners, as required under law.
Pursuant to such unauthorized action, the ADLR communicated the
outcome of the Phodi and Durasth exercise to the jurisdictional
Tahsildar, who, in blatant violation of the principles of natural justice,
proceeded to reduce an extent of 0.24 guntas from the land granted to
the Petitioners and arbitrarily added the same to the landholding of
another individual, allegedly favouring such third party without any
legal basis.

8. It is pertinent to note that the very same Tahsildar, who had
previously conducted the spot inspection and prepared the valid land
sketch dated 19.09.2024, now, acting without jurisdiction and under
apparent extraneous influence, prepared a second sketch unilaterally,
thereby diminishing the Petitioners’ landholding by 0.24 guntas,
without issuing any notice or order under due process of law. Copies
of the communication dated 18.02.2025 from the ADLR and the
sketch dated 18.05.2025, evidencing the illegal reduction of land and
reallocation, are produced herewith and marked as ANNEXURES-F
and G, respectively.
9. It is further submitted that, acting solely on the basis of the
communication issued by the Assistant Director of Land Records
(ADLR) and without issuing any prior notice or affording an
opportunity of hearing to the Petitioners, the Tahsildar of Gubbi
Taluk, in a patently arbitrary and illegal manner, proceeded to pass a
unilateral order, thereby reducing an extent of 0.24 guntas from the
land earlier granted to the Petitioners’ father. It is most pertinent to
bring to the attention of this Hon'ble Court that the very same
Tahsildar, who had earlier issued the Official Memorandum dated
22.11.2017 and the Saguvali Chit dated 01.01.2018 and Sketch dated
19.09.2024 in favour of the Petitioners’ father after due enquiry and
verification, has now, without any statutory authority or jurisdiction,
